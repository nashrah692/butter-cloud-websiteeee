so here's the math

a cake isn't one step. it's a chain of steps that each need their own time — skip one and the whole cake suffers. here's what actually happens between "order placed" and "cake ready":

  1. 1

    Mixing the batter

    25 min

    Sponge batter made from scratch, every single time.

  2. 2

    Baking the sponge

    35–40 min

    Low and slow, so it actually cooks through instead of just looking done.

  3. 3

    Cooling down

    ~60 min

    A warm sponge falls apart the moment you touch it — it needs time to set.

  4. 4

    Locking in the moisture

    a few hrs–overnight

    Cling wrapped and chilled so the sponge stays soft, not dry, by the time it's your cake.

  5. 5

    Soaking the sponge

    2+ hrs, or during coverage

    Three-milk sponges need at least 2 hours soaking before assembly. Simple sponges (pineapple, fudge, etc.) get soaked the moment coverage starts — no separate wait.

  6. 6

    Prepping the fillings

    15–20 min

    Whipping cream, lotus spread, chocolate spread, crushed biscuit — whatever your cake calls for, made fresh.

  7. 7

    Covering & finishing

    up to 30 min

    The actual decorating — piping, spreading, making it look like your cake.

~5 hrs

of hands-on work — and that's before some cakes go back in the fridge to chill overnight. that's the pre-order math.

quick answers before you order

How far in advance do I need to order?

Short version: at least 1 day. Head up to the Pre-Orders section for the full breakdown of why.

Do you take same-day or urgent orders?

Sometimes — but I need at least 6 hours' notice, no exceptions. That's the minimum it actually takes to bake, cool, and finish a cake properly. Message me first before assuming it's possible.

How do I actually place an order?

Through WhatsApp, the order form on this site, or Instagram DM — whichever's easiest for you.

What sizes do your cakes come in?

Bento, 1 pound, 2 pound, and 3.5 pound. How many people that feeds really depends on how much your crowd eats, so go with your gut on portion size.

Can I customize the flavor or design?

Right now I'm focused on perfecting my craft, so simple customizations — flavor swaps, basic design tweaks — I'm your person. For fully elaborate, fancy custom designs, you'll want to look elsewhere for now.

Do you do eggless cakes or cater to dietary restrictions?

Not at the moment — but that might change down the line.

What payment methods do you accept?

Meezan Bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, SadaPay, or NayaPay.

Do you deliver, or is it pickup only?

Both — delivery and pickup are available, but only within Multan for now.

Can I cancel or change my order after placing it?

You've got a 1-hour window after placing your order to make changes. After that, it's locked in — so it's worth double checking everything within that hour.

Why should I trust a home bakery over a proper cake shop?

Anyone who's tried these cakes doesn't just order once — they come back. Every recipe here has been tested, redone, and perfected by hand until it's actually right, not just good enough. That's not a sales pitch, that's just what repeat customers look like.
